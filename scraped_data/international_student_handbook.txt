PRESIDENCY UNIVERSITY - BENGALURU
Office of International Affairs (OIA) (cid:127) Official International Student Handbook
1. Welcome & Orientation
Welcome to Presidency University, Bengaluru. The Office of International Affairs (OIA) is dedicated to supporting
international students throughout their academic journey in India. Upon your arrival at Kempegowda International
Airport (BLR), the university provides airport reception and transportation to the university campus upon prior request.
2. Mandatory Visa (S-Visa) & e-FRRO Compliance
International students must arrive strictly on an Indian Student Visa (S-Visa) endorsing Presidency University. In
compliance with Government of India regulations, all foreign national students holding visas exceeding 180 days must
complete online registration with the Foreigners Regional Registration Office (e-FRRO) within 14 days of landing. The
OIA desk issues the mandatory Bonafide Certificate and Form C required for your e-FRRO application.
3. Academic Structure & Credit Transfers
Degrees are recognized by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) and accredited bodies. Students participating
in dual-degree programs, semester abroad exchanges, and MoU initiatives (including Taiwan Education Center, DHIK
German Consortium, and partner universities in the USA, UK, and Europe) are advised to complete credit mapping
with their respective faculty advisors.
4. Hostel Accommodation & Campus Amenities
Furnished on-campus hostels feature round-the-clock Wi-Fi, biometric security, laundry services, and 24/7 power
backup. The campus dining facility caters to diverse palates offering vegetarian, non-vegetarian, and international
food options. All rooms comply with national safety standards.
5. Medical Healthcare & Insurance
Every foreign student is mandated to carry a comprehensive international health and hospitalization insurance policy
covering a minimum of USD 10,000 / INR 5,00,000. An on-campus medical center staffed with certified physicians is
available for routine examinations and emergency care, supported by tertiary hospital partnerships nearby.

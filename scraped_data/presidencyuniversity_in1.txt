Presidency University Signs MoU with Miami University for Global Academic Collaboration

 22-May-2025
Presidency University signed an MoU with Miami University, Ohio, USA, on May 22, 2025. The delegation was led by Dr Elizabeth Reitz Mullenix,
Provost and Executive Vice President; Dr. Vaishali Raval, Professor of Psychology; and Ms. Karla Guinigundo, Director, Global Partnerships Global
Initiatives.

The visit featured engaging discussions focused on advancing academic excellence, global engagement, and innovation. Key proposals included:
1. Centre for Teaching and Learning (CTL): A proposed collaborative hub for faculty development, joint research, pedagogical innovation, and academic
excellence.
2. Masterʼs Progression Pathways (3.5 + 1.5) and Direct Entry Programs: Planned pathways in computer science, engineering, and design to enable
seamless postgraduate transition to Miami University.
3. Dual PhD Programs: Potential collaboration in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Mechatronics, and allied fields to facilitate joint research and
supervision.
4. Hosting the Lilly Conference in India: Plans to co-host this prestigious faculty development conference, strengthening regional academic capacity.
The visit marked a significant milestone in the University's expanding global partnerships, reflecting a shared commitment to academic excellence,
global engagement, and innovative collaboration.

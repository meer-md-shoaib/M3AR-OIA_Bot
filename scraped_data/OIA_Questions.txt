1. Department Offerings & Academic Pathways
The Department of International Affairs facilitates global academic exposure for students across various
stages of their degree, including reciprocal exchange agreements, fast-track postgraduate articulations, short-
term experiential schools, and overseas corporate or research internships.

2. Question & Answer Breakdown (Part I)
Q1:What are the most frequently asked questions from students?
Students primarily inquire about international internships, exchange terms, and Master's pathways. The two most
recurrent follow-up concerns are: "Will I be able to secure employment in the destination country?" and "What level
of scholarship or ﬁnancial assistance is available?"
Q2:What information do new students often struggle to ﬁnd?
The department has not experienced issues where new students struggle to ﬁnd information, as core
documentation and advisories are regularly made accessible to entrants.

3. Question & Answer Breakdown (Part II)
Q3:What public resources and documentation are currently available?
Complete transparency is maintained via the Presidency University International webpage. It catalogs current
partner universities, exchange options, alumni who have traveled abroad, and visiting international delegates,
allowing prospective and enrolled students to conduct comprehensive research.
Q4:What are the department's working hours and communication channels?
Oﬃcial in-oﬃce working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Unoﬃcially, communication is active round the clock as
students frequently message faculty and coordinators on instant messaging channels late into the night.
Q5:Does program information change frequently, and what are the notice periods?
The oﬃce plans far in advance, giving at least six months' notice for regular student exchange and study
programs. Specialized or inbound internship calls may have tighter turnarounds, operating on approximately one
month's notice.
4. Speciﬁcations for AI Assistant & Chatbot Design
Strict Privacy Safeguards
Mandatory Human Veriﬁcation: When asked what
an AI system must never answer autonomously, the
oﬃcial emphasized student personal data. Personal
identiﬁers, student records, and application data
must remain strictly conﬁdential and never be
compromised or exposed without human review.
First-Year Orientation Focus
Target Audience: Junior/freshmen students lack full
awareness before formal orientations. An AI
assistant's highest value is offering 24/7 self-
service guidance regarding global eligibility,
application windows, and procedural checklists
before senior semester orientation takes place.
5. Departmental Recommendations for Students
Key Takeaways for Students & Bot Messaging:
Prioritize oﬃcial email broadcasts: While WhatsApp and informal word-of-mouth are common, veriﬁed notices and
criteria are delivered through oﬃcial university channels.
Independent decision making: Students are urged to apply based on their individual academic goals and genuine
interests, rather than simply following peer groups or friends abroad.

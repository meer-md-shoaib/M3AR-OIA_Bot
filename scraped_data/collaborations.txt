International Engagement
International Collaborations
Presidency University has established collaborations with more than 165 premier institutions across 33 countries for various academic and research cooperation. International collaborations provide the most strategic opportunities for the mutual benefit of the students and faculties of partnering universities, to pursue their academics in various countries across the globe.
50+ Countries International Collaborations
The Office of International Affairs (OIA) at Presidency University has been established with the primary objective of providing international exposure to every student at Presidency. OIA offers a range of opportunities and supports both students and faculty members in promoting internationalization at home.
This includes establishing new collaborations with renowned institutions across the globe as well as sustaining existing partnerships. OIA collaborates closely with various stakeholders within the institution and across its schools and departments to facilitate internationalization at home.
OIA Outreach & Activities
His Excellency Mr. Achim Burkart, Consul General, German Consulate General, Bengaluru, visited Presidency University on 27th of July 2022 for the MoU signing ceremony with DHIK, the German University Consortium for International Cooperations, and the release of the 1st International Bulletin of the Office of International Affairs.
OIA encourages, supports, and facilitates the department / schools in organizing various joint events, such as webinars, workshops, conferences, etc., in association with partner institutions abroad.
Interaction with successful students abroad is an initiation of OIA to bring 2nd, 3rd, and final year students at Presidency University under a common roof where current students can clarify their doubts about opportunities, lifestyle and cost of living, visa processing, higher education, and job opportunities after semester exchange programs/ research internships.
Presidency University is registered with The Study in India (SII) programme. This is a flagship initiative launched by the Ministry of Education (MoE), Government of India .
The program aims to position India as a leading educational destination for international students, inviting them to pursue higher education in the country. With its array of compelling features, the program offers international students the opportunity to experience the best of Indian education. It encourages students from around the world to explore valuable educational prospects offered by top universities in India.
The Study in India portal integrates dynamic features including student registration, generation of Unique Student ID, online application submission, visa processing and FRRO application process.https://studyinindia.gov.in/
